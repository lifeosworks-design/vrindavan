# VRINDAVAN — Brand & Legal Checklist
Direction "Quiet Mandir" is kept as provisionally accepted — **not redesigned here.** This checklist verifies ownership, licensing and claim risk. Nothing below is legal advice; items marked **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** need an IP attorney/CA.
Status: OPEN = unanswered. All items are currently OPEN because no documents were provided.

## 1. Logo & wordmark ownership
| # | Question | Why it matters | Evidence to collect | Status | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who created the logo set (emblem 1, circle emblem 2, wordmark)? Designer/agency/tool? | Ownership starts with the creator | Name, invoice, messages | OPEN | You |
| 2 | Is there a **written agreement** with the designer? Does it include a copyright **assignment** (not just a licence) and delivery of source files? | Without assignment the designer may keep copyright; trademark filing is weaker | Signed agreement / assignment deed | OPEN | You + attorney |
| 3 | If an **AI image generator** was used: which tool and plan? Do its terms allow commercial use and registration as a trademark? Is the output sufficiently original/distinctive? | AI-generated art can raise copyright, originality and similarity risks | Tool terms (dated), generation history | OPEN — **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** | You + attorney |
| 4 | Do the files contain any third-party elements (stock vectors, fonts, textures, existing temple/peacock/flute artwork)? | Embedded licensed elements may not be transferable | Source files + asset list | OPEN | Designer |
| 5 | Are vector masters, flat one-colour wordmark and a favicon derivative available? | Needed for small sizes; derivatives must be authorised | Files + confirmation | OPEN | Designer |
| 6 | Does the emblem resemble an existing mark/temple/organisation logo (e.g., Krishna-themed organisations)? | Conflict/infringement risk | Vienna-class device search, attorney view | BLOCKED (OTP/captcha) — **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** | You + attorney |
| 7 | Name "VRINDAVAN": place-name distinctiveness and register crowding; "WAVES OF VRINDAVAN" class 35 overlap (aggregator lead) | Registrability and conflict | IP India search results + attorney opinion | BLOCKED — **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** | You + attorney |
| 8 | Domain, social handles, business-name registration, Google Business listing | Brand protection | Availability checks (not purchased) | OPEN | You |

## 6. Brand gate rule
Brand status becomes **VERIFIED** only when: logo ownership/assignment is documented (or the logo is re-drawn/acquired cleanly); the trademark search report and attorney view exist; fonts/images/icons have recorded licences; supplier-photo permissions are in writing (or we own all imagery); and the Devanagari strings are reviewed. Until then: **PARTIAL**. Do not order printed packaging, run ads under the name, or buy domains/handles in bulk before this gate.
