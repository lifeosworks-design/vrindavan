### 1. Brass
Our products: brass Radha-Krishna, Lakshmi–Ganesha, Ganesha, Hanuman/Shiva idols; possibly brass lamps/diyas/bells/thalis later.
1. Is a brass deity figure an "idol of metal" (S.No. 293) or a "statuette/ornament of base metal" (8306, S.No. 420)? Which HSN goes on the invoice? Is the rate 5% either way, inter-State and intra-State?
2. Does any condition apply (hand-made/handicraft, supplier registration, declaration)? What proof should we hold?
3. Are brass articles that are *not* figures (lamps, thalis, bells, dhoop burners) under 7419 (18%), 7418, 8306 or another heading? Rate?
4. Does "decorative" use (e.g., Buddha for décor) change anything for brass?
5. Antique/lacquered/plated or painted finish: does it change HSN/rate (we saw sources mentioning 18% for plated items)?

### 2. Other metal (copper, bronze, white metal / zinc alloy, aluminium, panchaloha)

### 3. Marble (real marble)

2. Is there an equivalent exemption for **inter-State** supplies (IGST 10/2025 — please confirm the entry and wording)?
3. **Decorative** marble figures (non-deity, e.g., Buddha décor or animals): rate?
4. If we sell exempt and taxable goods: ITC reversal/Section 17(2) impact on inputs, freight, packaging, software; invoice formats; return-filing treatment.
5. Wooden-crate packing and freight for marble: GST on freight charged to customer?

### 4. Stone (other than marble: sandstone, granite, soapstone, black stone)

2. Garden/decor statues of stone: rate?

### 5. Wood
1. Wooden deities (S.No. 124 exempt?) vs wooden statuettes/ornaments (4420, 5%): how to classify a carved wooden Ganesha used in a home mandir?

### 6. Resin / polyresin / cold-cast
1. Which HSN applies to resin/polyresin/cold-cast idols — 3926 (Sched. II, 18%), statuettes under another heading, or an "idol" entry? We found **no** entry naming resin idols.

### 7. Marble composite ("marble dust + resin", "marble powder")

2. Can it be invoiced as "marble"? (Also a consumer-law/claims question.)

### 8. Other composite (fibreglass, plaster of Paris, terracotta/clay, papier-mâché, mixed materials)
