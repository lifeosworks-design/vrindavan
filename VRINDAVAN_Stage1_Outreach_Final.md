## Message B — Detailed qualification (send to suppliers who answered A positively; email)
**Subject:** Partnership enquiry — written confirmation of dropship terms ([material] murtis)
> Dear [Name],
> Thank you for your reply. To decide whether we can work together, please confirm the following **in writing** (a table or numbered reply is fine):
> **1. Catalogue & pricing** — product catalogue; wholesale price per item (ex-GST and incl. GST); GST rate and HSN you invoice under; MOQ per item; price validity.
> **2. Product data** — dimensions, **actual weight**, and **packed weight / box size (volumetric weight)** per item; material (alloy / resin type / marble type) and finish; whether the piece is hand-finished, cast or moulded.
> **3. Dropshipping & branding** — do you ship single orders directly to our customers? Is **blind shipping** possible (no supplier name, branding or contact on parcel/invoice)? Packaging options: plain, custom box, insert card; **private/white-label** and customisation possibilities, with cost and MOQ.
> **4. Dispatch & logistics** — dispatch time after order (working days); courier partners; your shipping rate by weight/zone; whether you share the tracking number the same day.
> **5. COD & RTO** — do you support COD, and what is the fee and remittance cycle? Who pays for RTO and what happens to returned stock?
> **6. Quality & loss** — packing method for fragile pieces; policy for damaged-in-transit, defective and wrong items; **replacement** timeline; return policy.
> **7. Operations** — how do we send orders (WhatsApp / Excel / API / Shopify)? How do you update **inventory**? What happens on stock-outs?
> **8. Compliance & content** — GSTIN and invoice format; product **photos and videos** of the exact piece and permission to use them on our store.
> **9. Samples** — sample order process, price, shipping and lead time for [3] items.
> Please also share your registered business name and workshop address. Regards, [Name · phone]
