# VRINDAVAN — Sample Test Protocol (100-point scoring)
Use for every pilot sample (SKUs C1–C3, B1–B2, E1–E3). You perform the physical steps; bring the scorecard, photos and tracking data back for scoring.

## 0. Principle
**A beautiful product that arrives damaged is a COMMERCIAL FAILURE, not a "packaging issue".** Damage in transit fails the SKU–supplier–packaging combination until it is repacked and re-tested with zero damage.

## 1. Setup (before samples arrive)
- Prepare: digital kitchen scale (±1 g), ruler/calipers, magnet, phone camera (daylight + warm lamp), notebook/printout, a clean white sheet, a flat shelf for the stability test.
- Ask the supplier for tracking number, dispatch date and pre-dispatch photos/video of the exact piece and packed parcel (Message C).
- Run **two shipping legs**: (1) the sample shipped to you (supplier's own packing), (2) a **second unit** (or the same piece re-packed in the proposed production packaging) shipped via the real courier to a test address in a different city/zone. Include one COD parcel to a friend if possible.
- Record the parcel's **unopened** photos from all sides before opening.

## 3. Hard rejection thresholds (any one = FAIL regardless of score)
1. **Any damage in transit** to any parcel in the test set (crack, chip, bent part, scratched finish, wet/torn parcel) — repack and retest; two damage events = supplier fail.
2. Critical defect: crack, missing/loose part, wrong deity, face damage, unstable base.
3. **Material mismatch**: claimed brass/marble not consistent with tests (magnet attracts → not brass; weight/density grossly off; "marble" that is resin/composite).
4. Weight or dimensions off by **> 10%** from the listing (or > 5% without an explanation).
5. Paint/plating that rubs off or transfers colour.
6. No GST tax invoice with GSTIN, or invoice name differs from the registered business.
7. Supplier refuses (or will not put in writing) damage replacement, blind shipping, or sample re-ship.
8. Dispatch > 5 working days, or no tracking/AWB shared.
9. Unboxing score < 3/5 or testers' "looks cheaper than its price" ≥ 2 of 3.
10. Packaging requires supplier branding that cannot be removed (if white-label was promised).

## 4. Pass bands
- **≥ 80 and no hard fail** → PASS (eligible for the pilot).
- **70–79, no hard fail** → CONDITIONAL — fix the named weak items and retest.
- **< 70 or any hard fail** → FAIL (SKU–supplier–packaging combination).
- Each category must score ≥ 60% of its maximum to pass.
- Gate "Product Quality" = ≥ 3 SKUs (Core/Backup) PASS from at least one supplier **and** zero damaged parcels across the shipping legs.

## 5. Evidence to bring back
Scorecard (this table filled), unopened-parcel photos, unboxing photos/video, macro photos, scale/ruler photos, invoice (redact your address if you want), AWB number + courier tracking screenshots, supplier chat/emails showing replacement/return terms, tester ratings (3 people), and the actual cost you paid (price + packing + shipping + any fees).
## 6. Retest rules
After any fix (repack, different supplier batch), ship **two more** units through the same courier/zone; the combination must pass with zero damage before it counts.
